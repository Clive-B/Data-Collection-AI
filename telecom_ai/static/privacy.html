Data use notice

Privacy and analytical boundaries

This service processes questions against an approved telecom reporting database. It returns only the observations, aggregates, source references, and quality warnings required to answer a question.

Information processed

Question text, selected analytical parameters, aggregated telecom observations, and minimized audit metadata may be processed. Do not submit subscriber-level information, credentials, or personal data.

Embedded NCA Adinkra Omega Copilot

When you ask the embedded copilot, your question and a minimized subset of aggregate analytical evidence are sent server-to-server to the OpenAI Responses API. The OpenAI API key remains on the server and is never sent to browser code. Requests set response storage to false, although applicable OpenAI abuse-monitoring and data-control terms may still apply.

The local deterministic engine remains the numerical source of truth. Governance holds, credential-disclosure requests, and live-network action requests are not sent to OpenAI. Deployment owners must approve the hosting region, OpenAI project controls, permitted data classes, and any cross-border transfer basis before production activation.

Human authority

Results support analysis but do not authorize regulatory enforcement or live-network changes. A designated human remains responsible for consequential decisions.

Current enforcement status

The local development server does not itself prove production TLS, African data residency, encryption at rest, or external audit enforcement. Those controls must be implemented and independently verified during deployment.
